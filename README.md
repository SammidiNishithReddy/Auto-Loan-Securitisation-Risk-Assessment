# 📖 Project Overview

The **Auto Loan Securitisation Risk Assessment Dashboard** is an interactive **Power BI** solution developed to analyze an auto loan portfolio from multiple business perspectives.

The dashboard enables financial institutions, analysts, and investors to monitor:

- Portfolio Performance
- Credit Risk
- Delinquency Trends
- IFRS 9 Expected Credit Loss (ECL)
- Collections & Recovery
- Vintage Performance
- Investor Reporting

The objective is to provide actionable insights for risk monitoring, portfolio management, and investment decision-making.

---

# 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze portfolio health
- Monitor borrower credit quality
- Evaluate delinquency trends
- Calculate Expected Credit Loss (IFRS 9)
- Assess collections and recoveries
- Perform vintage analysis
- Generate investor reports
- Support risk-based decision making

# 📊 Dashboard Pages

## 1️⃣ Executive Portfolio Dashboard

Provides a high-level summary of the loan portfolio.

### KPIs

- Total Customers
- Portfolio Outstanding
- Collection Efficiency
- Default Rate
- Expected Credit Loss
- Total Collections

---

## 2️⃣ Credit Risk Dashboard

Analyzes borrower credit quality using:

- Average CIBIL Score
- Loan-to-Value Ratio (LTV)
- Debt-to-Income Ratio (DTI)
- Average Interest Rate

---

## 3️⃣ Delinquency Dashboard

Monitors overdue loans through:

- DPD Buckets
- Overdue Amount
- Cure Rate
- Repossessions
- Write-Offs

---

## 4️⃣ IFRS 9 Expected Credit Loss Dashboard

Displays:

- Stage 1 Exposure
- Stage 2 Exposure
- Stage 3 Exposure
- Expected Credit Loss (ECL)
- Provision Analysis

---

## 5️⃣ Collections & Recovery Dashboard

Includes:

- Collections
- Recoveries
- Recovery Rate
- Net Loss
- Collection Efficiency

---

## 6️⃣ Vintage Analysis Dashboard

Analyzes portfolio performance over time using:

- Pool Factor
- Net Loss Rate
- Prepayments
- Vintage Curves

---

## 7️⃣ Investor Reporting Dashboard

Tracks investor-focused metrics:

- Beginning of Period (BOP) Balance
- End of Period (EOP) Balance
- Gross Loss
- Net Loss
- CPR
- SMM
- HHI
- Collection Trigger

---

# 📈 Key Business Insights

- Healthy portfolio performance with strong collections.
- Low default rate indicates good borrower quality.
- Majority of exposure remains in IFRS 9 Stage 1.
- Strong recovery operations reduce portfolio losses.
- Vintage analysis shows predictable loan amortization.
- Investor reporting metrics indicate stable portfolio performance.
